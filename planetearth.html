Planet Earth-Quick Guide

Our planet is full of incredible records, species, and facts. Here is a quick guide using lists:

Steps to protect our planet

  1. Reduce your carbon footprint
  2. Conserve water and energy
  3. Support renewable energy initiatives
  4. Minimize waste and recycle
  5. Protect natural habitats

Incredible natural wonders

  • Grand Canyon, USA
  • Great Barrier Reef, Australia
  • Mount Everest, Nepal
  • Amazon Rainforest, South America
  • Victoria Falls, Zambia/Zimbabwe
